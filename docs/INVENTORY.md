# Phase 0 inventory

Counts, page numbers and text-layer share only; no outcome and no share of any codebook category has been seen. No document text was stored or printed. The only text-level steps are the per-page character count (text layer) and, for FDA Integrated Reviews, a yes/no test of bookmarks or heading-like lines for the section-title words non-clinical and pharmacology/toxicology. Generated by `studies/nam/inventory/report.py` from raw data kept outside the repository.

A text layer does not mean good text quality (scanned, redacted or edited reviews can still carry a text layer). Quality is measured in the pilot.

## Terms for reuse and publication of derived data

- FDA: "may be republished, reprinted and otherwise used freely by anyone" ([FDA website policies](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/about-website/website-policies)). Applies to fda.gov content 'unless otherwise noted'; the review PDFs are partly redacted agency documents and may contain third-party material. Derived data (coding, quotes, links) is not addressed explicitly.

- EMA: "for non-commercial and commercial purposes, provided that EMA is always acknowledged as the source" ([EMA legal notice](https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/about-us/about-website/legal-notice)). Third-party material is excluded. Derived data is not addressed explicitly; acknowledging EMA as the source is required.

Both permit reuse with attribution; whether quotes of reasonable length and coding tables count as permitted reuse is a reading of these terms, not a legal opinion.

## Open for decision

1. **EMA definition B over-counts by about 16 % against EMA's own counts** (+69 on 431, 2015-2025, counted by opinion year; 41 B rows have no opinion date, so the gap is if anything larger). The causes are not examined row by row (a check of identical new-substance sets found few duplicates). Proposal: in the full run, extract the EPAR's own statement of whether the medicine has a new active substance (a metadata field, not an outcome) and use it as the final unit definition. B then becomes a candidate list only. The 2021 EMA figure (54) still needs a manual check.

2. **Scope: total pages in the universe.** Estimated from the sample mean pages per year and authority times the number of documents that year (FDA: drugs with a non-clinical document; EMA: drugs with a public EPAR). One document per drug only; n = 2 per stratum, so the figures are rough.

| Authority | Documents | Mean pages per document | Estimated pages | Approx. +-2 SE range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FDA | 541 | 229 | 124000 | 96000-152000 |
| EMA | 557 | 144 | 80000 | 71000-90000 |

About 204,000 pages in total for one non-clinical document per drug. Reading that volume with a model is large, so the coarse pre-filter allowed by extraction rule 4 (only if the pilot shows it loses no A findings) is needed.

3. **FDA 2015-2017 has a low text layer** (sample: 799 of 1267 pages, 63 %; per year 0.42, 0.79, 0.76; 110 drugs with a non-clinical document in those years). A decision on OCR is needed: OCR those documents, accept lower recall there, or exclude the years. Text quality after OCR is measured in the pilot.
